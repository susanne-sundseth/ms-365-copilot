# Using Copilot in Teams

This is feature is only available with CP for M365. See [Section 1 Intro](./1_intro.md).

Only you can see your CP during a meeting (unless you share your screen).

## Lesson 74 Fundamentals

The CP icon should be available after you click into messages.

Also, go to Apps within Teams and search for Copilot. Open the app called M365 Chat.

Ensure that each meeting that you set up through Teams is CP enabled.

* When you create a new meeting, select **Option > More Options**. Then, select _Without Transcription_ for the Copilot setting. This means that CP is available to you without CP needing to transcribe the call for you.

    CP is only available to people within your organization who have a CP license.

* If you do not have _Without transcription_ available to you (_with transacription_ is the only option), ensure that transcription is enabled for meetings. Contact your administrator. If you are the administrator, log in to https://admin.teams.microsoft.com and the select **Meetings > Meeting policies** to update the Global policy to enable transcription or create a NewPolicy that enable transcription for certain users.

## Lesson 75 Summarize and ask questions during meetings

You must be in the convo interface to be able to use CP to summarize a convo thread. Click link for _# replies from participant A and whoever_, or click **... > Open conversation in new Window**.

In CP, you can select **Highlights from the Convo**. The highlights will include reference links so you can readily access that part of the convo.

![convo highlights](./assets/teams_convo_highlights.png)

You can then provide additional prompts if needed, such as _What decisions where made_, _Any approvals needed from me?_ CP knows who you are and will provide the appropriate response. You can also ask for info in a table, such as _Place any action items and their due dates in a table_.

## Lesson 76-77 Catch up with meetings and ask questions

CP accesses the transcript of the meeting so if you are late to a meeting, you can ask CP to _Recap the meeting so far_ to catch up.

You can also ask specific questions:

* Were micro-influencers discussed yet?
* What does Einar think about closing the Manitou Springs location?
* What is Becca's take on influencer marketing?
* What are the key dates and deadlines mentioned?
* What percentage of Europe uses TikTok?

    In this case, the response would cite the transcript (Henry said that 50 percentage of Europeans use TikTok) but then indicate that the information was not verified or supported from other sources so it might not be correct.
* Create a table with the ideas discussed and their pros and cons.

## Lesson 78 Get meeting coaching

Use CP to be a better meeting participant.

You can ask questions about another participants input. For example, if micro-influencers were discussed by Jhing:

* What are issues with Jhing's propsal to use micro-influencers?
* What are additional benefits to using micro-influencers that Jhing did not discuss?
* What are some holes in Jhing's discussion regarding mico-influencers?
* What are some good points to make the argument that micro-influencing is a bad idea?
* Suggest follow-up questions

  The response will indicate who to direct the questions to.

* What questions can I ask to move the meeting forward.
